# SimuVolt-Privacy-Policy

SimuVolt Privacy Policy
Last updated: 31 October 2026



[Hmam Kaadna] ("we") makes SimuVolt. This policy says what the App collects and why. Contact: [info.hourstracker@gmail.com].

What we collect
Without an account: nothing leaves your device. Projects, settings and language are stored locally.

With an account (optional):

Data	Why	Where
Email address and password	Sign-in. The password is stored only as a hash by our auth provider.	Supabase, Frankfurt (eu-central-1)
Your saved projects (circuit drawings, names, part and wire counts, save time)	Sync between your devices	Supabase, Frankfurt
Sign-in session tokens	Keep you signed in	On your device
We do not collect your location, contacts, photos, advertising identifiers or analytics, and we do not track you across other apps or sites. We do not sell or share your data for advertising.

Who processes it
Supabase Inc. hosts the database and sign-in service on our behalf. Access to each project row is restricted by row level security to the account that owns it.

Retention and deletion
We keep your data while your account exists. In the App open the profile screen and choose Delete account to permanently remove your account and all of your cloud projects immediately. Data held only on your device is removed when you delete the App.

Your rights
Depending on where you live you may have the right to access, correct, export or delete your data, or to object to its processing. Email [CONTACT EMAIL] and we will respond within 30 days.

Children
SimuVolt is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect their data.

Security
Data is sent over HTTPS. No system is perfectly secure; keep your password private.

Changes
We will update the date above when this policy changes.
